# Checklist Taxonomy

## Sorting rule

Every item gets exactly:
- one primary tab/category
- one section

It may also have multiple tags.

Do not duplicate the same physical item just because it is relevant to several activities.

---

# Food

## Breakfast
Examples:
- eggs
- bacon
- sausage
- pancake mix
- syrup
- oatmeal
- cereal
- breakfast bars

## Lunch
Examples:
- sandwich bread
- deli meat
- cheese
- wraps
- lunch kits

## Dinner
Examples:
- burgers
- hot dogs
- buns
- steaks
- camp meals
- pasta
- canned meals

## Snacks
Examples:
- chips
- trail mix
- jerky
- fruit
- granola bars
- marshmallows

## Drinks
Examples:
- drinking water
- coffee
- tea
- drink mix
- soda

## Condiments
The former generic `sauce` item must not exist.

Use:
- mayonnaise
- ketchup
- BBQ sauce
- hot sauce

Other examples:
- mustard
- salt
- pepper
- cooking oil
- butter

## Staples / Ingredients
Examples:
- foil
- seasonings
- sugar
- shelf-stable ingredients

Note: non-food kitchen consumables such as paper towels or trash bags belong in Gear > Camp Kitchen, not Food.

## Power & Electronics
This section prevents ambiguous items from being mixed into Survival.

- charging cables
- wall / vehicle USB charger

- camera

## Fuel / Vehicle
Use only when relevant to the trip.

Gasoline is not the same as propane/stove fuel.

- toothbrush

- soap

- wet wipes
- towel
- deodorant

- feminine hygiene products as applicable
- contact / glasses supplies as applicable

## Sun & Bugs
- sunscreen

## First Aid
- first-aid kit
- bandages
- gauze

- blister care

# Extras

This is a staging area.

Examples:
- campsite-specific amenities
- convenience upgrades
- family/pet-specific gear
- activity-specific gear
- items suggested by research but not universally needed
- newly created trip items not yet promoted to the master inventory

Extras should support a `Promote to Master Inventory` action after a trip.
